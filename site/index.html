Our story

Built by family, open to everyone

Ultima Warriors started with a dad and his son gaming together and realizing there weren't many places online built for us — players who've been holding a controller since the cartridge days, have jobs and families, and just want to log on, have a laugh, and play with people who respect each other.

So we built one. Our Discord is the heart of the community, and this site is the central hub for everywhere else you can find us.
