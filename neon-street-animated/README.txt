ULTIMA WARRIORS - NEON STREET (animated desktop wallpaper)  v1.2
Made for 5120 x 1440 (fills any screen). Works offline, nothing to install.

WHAT'S ALIVE
  - ULTIMA WARRIORS neon sign on the steel frame: tubes ignite one by one at
    start-up, hum, light up the wall, and the tired "R" stutters now and then.
    Every couple of minutes the whole sign browns out for a blink.
  - Neon clock + date in a steel housing (uses your PC's time).
  - Neon in the street breathes and buzzes, steam rolls from the vents, rain
    falls (and picks up the sign's colour), puddles splash, haze drifts, and
    air traffic crosses the gaps in the skyline.

SET IT UP (Windows) - pick one:
  Lively Wallpaper (free, Microsoft Store)
    1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (e.g. Documents\UltimaWarriors-Neon).
    2. Lively -> "+" (Add wallpaper) -> choose index.html in this folder.
  Wallpaper Engine (Steam)
    1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent.
    2. "Create Wallpaper" (bottom left) -> open index.html.
       Settings panel (right side): show clock, 24-hour clock, and on/off
       switches for flicker & flashes, haze, film grain, rain, steam,
       plus low quality.
  Just to look at it: double-click index.html, then press F11.

OPTIONS (add to the end of the address, e.g. index.html?h24=1&fps=30)
    ?h24=1         24-hour clock
    ?clock=0       hide the clock
    ?fps=30        cap the frame rate (saves power)
    ?quality=low   less rain/steam, for slower PCs
    ?flicker=0     no flicker or flashes (sign stutter/dimming, buzzing neon)
    ?haze=0        no haze/fog        ?grain=0   no film grain
    ?rain=0        no rain            ?steam=0   no steam

FILES
    index.html       the wallpaper page
    cyber.js         sign, clock, rain, steam, haze, lights
    assets-data.js   the street image and the neon font, embedded
    project.json / LivelyInfo.json   settings for Wallpaper Engine / Lively
    still-5120x1440.jpg   a plain still picture, if you just want a static background

IF WALLPAPER ENGINE CRASHES OR SHOWS BLACK
  1. Remove the old copy first: in Wallpaper Engine, right-click the old
     "Ultima Warriors - Neon Street" -> Unsubscribe/Delete, then create it again
     from this v1.2 folder (it copies files when you create it, so the old,
     crashing version would otherwise stay).
  2. Make sure you picked index.html from the UNZIPPED folder, not inside the zip.
  3. Try low quality: Wallpaper Engine settings panel -> tick "Low quality".
  4. If something still goes wrong, a yellow error message appears in the
     bottom-left of the wallpaper. Send a photo/screenshot of it.
  5. Lively Wallpaper (free) is a good fallback: it runs the same folder.
